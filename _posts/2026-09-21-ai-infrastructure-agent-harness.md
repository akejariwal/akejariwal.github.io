Progress in AI has depended on the infrastructure that prepares data and supports training. Agent harnesses bring a similar systems 
challenge into execution: assembling context, coordinating tools, and verifying outcomes. How much can these choices improve performance 
with the model held fixed? In the attached essay, I explore this parallel, examine recent results, and break down what can be optimized 
across the harness. I also consider where these changes could help open-weight models narrow task-specific gaps with frontier systems.
